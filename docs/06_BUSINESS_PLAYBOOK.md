# TRQX Business Playbook

## Daily Workflow

1. Review market conditions.
2. Prepare the market thesis.
3. Review economic events.
4. Review earnings.
5. Publish the morning briefing.
6. Update Discord.
7. Review customer messages.
8. Review platform health.
9. Review marketing content.
10. Record outstanding tasks.

## Weekly Workflow

- Review customer activity.
- Review churn and subscriptions.
- Review content performance.
- Review simulator usage.
- Add or improve lessons.
- Record one or more product videos.
- Review the product roadmap.
- Back up production data.
